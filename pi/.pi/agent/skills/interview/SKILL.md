Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until we reach a shared understanding. Walk through the problem resolving dependencies between decisions. For each question, provide your recommended answer.

the goal is to plan the feature narrowly and precisely without adding to the scope of the task.

Ask the questions in related chunks no more than 5 at a time.

If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase, explore the codebase or documentation instead.
